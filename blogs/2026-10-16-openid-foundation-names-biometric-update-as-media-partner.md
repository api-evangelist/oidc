The OpenID Foundation is pleased to announce Biometric Update as the official media partner for the forthcoming OpenID Well-Known Conference. Taking place in London on February 2-4, 2027, this is the OpenID Foundation’s first global conference. Its goal is to help digital identity and credential ecosystems move from implementation to widespread adoption.
